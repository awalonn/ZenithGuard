What's New in ZenithGuard

v2.2.0

Version 2.2.0 - The Fortress Update

Firefox Support

ZenithGuard is now truly cross-platform! We have achieved **Full Compatibility with Mozilla Firefox (Gecko)**. Enjoy the same advanced AI protection on your favorite privacy-focused browser.

Anti-Tamper & Keep-Alive

We've added a robust **Tamper Detector** that instantly restores the extension's interface if hostile scripts try to remove it. Plus, a new **Service Worker Heartbeat** ensures our AI engine never sleeps during critical analysis tasks.

Version 2.1.0 - Usability & Power

Dashboard Overhaul

A completely redesigned **Filter Lists** section makes it clear which rules are Static (Network) and which are Dynamic (Cosmetic). Real-time rule counters show exactly how many filters are active.

Manual Blocklist

You can now manually add specific domains to your **Network Blocklist** directly from the settings page, giving you granular control over what gets blocked.
